MATISSE is a European HORIZON-KDT-JU research project bringing together over 30
partners from 7 countries to develop an advanced framework for efficient engineering and validation
of industrial systems using Digital Twins (DTs). By integrating DTs with model-based, data-driven,
and cloud technologies, MATISSE aims to simulate, test, and predict system behaviors, enhancing
both productivity and quality. This innovative approach helps companies optimize their industrial
processes, reduce errors, and boost productivity, ultimately simplifying complex operations, like
machinery production and factory management.
