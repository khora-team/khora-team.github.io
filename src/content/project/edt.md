EDT is a program that ambitions to develop the required foundations of the science and engineering
of digital twins to propose the principles, tools and methods to support digital twin development,
validation and verification, deployment, maintenance and evolution.  
KHORA will be involved in the coordination of the
program, the coordination of one PC (PC5, called Genuine), and will participate to the different
PCs (PC3, PC5) through the supervision of PhD students. Genuine, Engineering Human-Digital Twin
Interactions, aims at proposing new techniques to ease the creation of highly interactive DTs.
