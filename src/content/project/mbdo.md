MBDO aims at providing the foundations for a Model-Based DevOps approach unifying
the different forms of models in the specific context of cloud-native and IoT systems. The proposed
Model-Based DevOps approach would then allow engineers to smoothly go back and forth from
Dev time to Ops time by leveraging semi-automatically generated digital twins of their systems.
