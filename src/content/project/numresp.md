The project aims to carefully study individuals’ practices and requirements in order to
challenge the emergence of environmental-aware methodologies and potential technologies. The
outcome of the project would consist in recommendations and specifications to build maintenance
processes and tools for software eco-design, evaluation of hardware-induced constraints due to
software architecture scenarios.
