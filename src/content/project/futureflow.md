In the context of Headwater catchments (HWC) and scientific computing, the FutureFlow
project aims at transferring innovations from software engineering in model switching and multi-
fidelity to the field of HWC hydrology. It proposes to develop an innovative modelling framework
that leverages existing as well as newly developed models of different fidelities and automatically
deploys them to various geomorphic and geologic contexts, using multiple data sources for model
parameterization and allowing automated model uncertainty assessment.
