The main objective of the CLEM project is the development of a tool-supported method-
ology that combines live and exploratory modeling (LEM) for behavioral modeling languages.
Specifically, we will explore and provide concepts and techniques for LEM on a generic level using
language workbenches, thus enabling LEM for all modeling languages created with them.
