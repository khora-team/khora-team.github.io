The AIM-PRO project aims to boost AI literacy across higher education and vocational
training by providing innovative tools, frameworks, and resources rooted in Open Education, Gener-
ative AI, and Digital Twin technology. It addresses the urgent need to integrate AI competencies into
diverse educational and professional contexts, ensuring inclusivity and adaptability for all learners,
regardless of their formal qualifications. By fostering multidisciplinary collaboration and deep
tech expertise, AIM-PRO bridges education, research, and industry to create resilient, AI-literate
communities. The project also emphasizes ethical AI deployment, environmental awareness, and
entrepreneurial skills to build inclusive and forward-thinking education systems in Europe.
