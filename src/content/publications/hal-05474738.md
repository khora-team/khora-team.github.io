The GI @ ICSE 2025 workshop, held 27 April, in addition to presentations, contained a keynote on the latest results on automated program generation for testing static program analyzers, which could have huge impact on GI for improving programs for APG, and a tutorial on Magpie, a widely used language independent GI tool. We summarise these, the papers, people, prizes, acknowledgements, discussions and hopes for the future.
